# Employee Leave Policy (SAMPLE)

## Casual Leave
Employees are entitled to 8 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward and no more than 3 days may be taken consecutively.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Earned Leave
Employees earn 1.5 days of leave per month of service, up to a maximum accumulation of 60 days. Applications must be submitted at least 7 days in advance.

## Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Employees may apply up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.

## Leave Applications
All leave must be applied for through the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager. Unapproved absence of more than 3 days is treated as unauthorised absence.
