# Library Policy (SAMPLE)

## Borrowing Limits
Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books for 14 days. Postgraduate students may borrow up to 6 books for 21 days. Faculty may borrow up to 10 books for a semester.

## Late Fines
Overdue books are fined Rs 2 per day per book. Borrowing privileges are suspended once the total fine exceeds Rs 200 until it is paid.

## Lost Books
A lost or damaged book must be replaced with the same edition or paid for at the current market price plus a Rs 100 processing fee.

## Digital Resources
Students can access e-journals and e-books through the library portal using their campus login, on or off campus. Sharing login credentials is prohibited.

## Study Rooms
Group study rooms can be booked for up to 2 hours per day through the library desk. Bookings not claimed within 15 minutes are released.
