# Data Protection Policy (SAMPLE - replace with real documents)

## Data Collection
Personal data is collected only for specified purposes and with the data subject's consent. Only the minimum data needed for the stated purpose may be collected.

## Data Retention
Student records are retained for 7 years after graduation. Personal data collected for events or surveys must be deleted within 90 days of the event unless consent for longer retention is recorded.

## Breach Reporting
Any suspected personal data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours of discovery. The Officer notifies affected individuals within 72 hours if the breach poses a risk to them.

## Access Requests
An individual may request a copy of their personal data from the Data Protection Officer. The request is answered within 30 days, and the first request in a year is free of charge.
