# Examination Rules (SAMPLE)

## Hall Ticket
Candidates must carry their hall ticket and a valid photo ID to every examination. Entry without a hall ticket is not permitted.

## Prohibited Items
Mobile phones, smartwatches, notes and unauthorised calculators are prohibited in the examination hall. Possession is treated as malpractice even if unused.

## Late Entry
Candidates may enter up to 30 minutes after the exam begins. No candidate may leave during the first 45 minutes.

## Malpractice
Confirmed malpractice results in a zero in that paper and may lead to suspension for one semester. Repeat offences lead to dismissal.

## Makeup Exams
A makeup exam is granted only for documented medical emergencies, with the application submitted within 3 days of the missed exam.
