## Attendance
Students must maintain minimum 75% attendance in each course to be eligible for end-semester examinations. A shortfall of up to 10% may be condoned once per academic year with a medical certificate submitted to the Dean of Academic Affairs.

## Course Withdrawal
A student may withdraw from a course before the withdrawal deadline by submitting form W-1 to the registrar. Withdrawal after the deadline is not permitted except on medical grounds. A grade of W is recorded and does not affect CGPA.

## Grade Appeals
A grade appeal must be filed with the course instructor within 7 days of result declaration. If unresolved within 10 working days, it may be escalated to the Appeals Committee, which decides within 15 working days.

## Re-evaluation
Re-evaluation of an end-semester answer script may be requested within 5 days of result declaration on payment of Rs 500 per course. The re-evaluated mark replaces the original only if it is higher.
