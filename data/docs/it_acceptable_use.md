# IT Acceptable Use Policy (SAMPLE)

## Account Security
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 90 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all accounts. Passwords must never be shared.

## Prohibited Activities
Users must not access illegal content, run port scanners, mine cryptocurrency, or use campus networks to distribute copyrighted material.

## Software Installation
Only licensed or open-source software approved by the IT office may be installed on institute-owned devices. Users cannot disable antivirus software.

## Incident Reporting
Lost devices and suspected security incidents must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours of discovery.

## Wi-Fi Usage
Each user may connect up to 3 devices to campus Wi-Fi. Bandwidth-heavy streaming may be throttled during working hours.
