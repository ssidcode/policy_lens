# Hostel Rules (SAMPLE)

## Curfew
Residents must be inside the hostel by 10:00 pm on weekdays and 11:00 pm on weekends. Late entry requires prior written permission from the warden.

## Visitors
Visitors are allowed only in the common room between 10:00 am and 6:00 pm. Overnight guests are not permitted under any circumstances.

## Room Allocation
Rooms are allocated by seniority and academic year. Room changes may be requested once per semester through the warden's office and are subject to availability.

## Mess Fees
Mess fees are Rs 4,500 per month, payable in advance by the 5th of each month. A late fee of Rs 100 applies after the due date.

## Room Damage
Residents are liable for damage to hostel property in their rooms. Repair costs are deducted from the security deposit of Rs 10,000.
