# Merit Scholarship Policy (SAMPLE)

## Eligibility
Students with a CGPA of 8.5 or above and a family income below Rs 8 lakh per year are eligible for the merit scholarship.

## Application Deadline
Applications must be submitted through the student portal by 31 August each year. Late applications are not considered.

## Renewal
The scholarship is renewed each year if the student maintains a CGPA of at least 8.0 and has no disciplinary record.

## Disbursement
Scholarship amounts are credited directly to the student's bank account in two instalments, in September and February.

## Disqualification
A scholarship is withdrawn immediately for submitting false documents or for any disciplinary penalty of suspension or higher.
